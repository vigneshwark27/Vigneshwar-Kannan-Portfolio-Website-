Problem addressed
Claims with missing or mismatched data are the single largest cause of TPA queries and resubmission delay.
User
Insurance desk coordinator, case manager.
Product intervention
Automatically cross-checks EMR, billing and insurance data against payer-specific rules before submission, surfacing blocking issues in real time instead of after a TPA rejection.
Expected outcome
Fewer first-pass queries, fewer resubmission cycles, faster time-to-submit.
